```
Iqra Island (جَزِيرَةُ اقْرَأْ) helps children who don't speak Arabic learn to read it, one letter at a time, while discovering the beauty of Islam. Everything is explained in simple English, with a friendly camel guide called Jamal.

📖 LEARN TO READ ARABIC
• All 28 letters: sounds, names, shapes and letter forms
• Tracing, listening, matching and word-building activities
• Vowels (fatḥa, kasra, ḍamma, sukūn, shadda) and first words
• Lessons adapt to your child's age: Little Seeds (3–5), Explorers (6–8), Adventurers (9–12) and Scholars (teens)

🗺️ A 3D JOURNEY
Walk across a 3D map from Makkah along the Hijrah Trail to Madinah, Al-Quds, Jeddah and AlUla, unlocking new lessons and stage quizzes on the way.

🌿 QUR'AN GARDEN
Listen to short surahs recited by Sheikh Mishary Alafasy with each word highlighted as it is recited. Slow it down, repeat an āyah, or switch on “my turn” to practise memorising.

⭐ STORIES & BOOKS
• Prophets' Stories with quizzes
• Book Tent: picture books about the prophets and the noble Companions (Abū Bakr, ʿUmar, ʿUthmān, ʿAlī, Bilāl, Khadījah, ʿĀʾishah and more)
• Following Islamic custom, prophets, companions and angels are never shown in pictures.

🕌 WUḌŪʾ, ṢALĀH, ḤAJJ & ʿUMRAH
Step-by-step wuḍūʾ and every prayer position, plus Ḥajj and ʿUmrah with Ṭawāf, Saʿī and Jamarāt mini-games and the Talbiyah.

🎮 GAMES THAT TEACH
• Arcade: 16 letter and word games
• Fun Park: Spot the Difference, Find It!, Noor Obby Run, Minaret Stack, Picture Puzzle and Masjid Builder
• Super Muslim: a platform adventure through 8 Islamic cities
• Noor Guardians: answer Islamic-knowledge questions to power up your jet
Children earn gold dinars in the Exam Hall and spend them on games, so learning always comes first.

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR PARENTS
• Several children on one device, each with their own progress
• A passcode-protected Grown-ups area with daily reports, game time limits and game prices
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no data collection
• Works offline: everything stays on your device

Made with love by a Muslim parent for children everywhere.
```
