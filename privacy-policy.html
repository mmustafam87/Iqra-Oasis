Iqra Island: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Iqra Island (“the app”) is an Arabic-reading and Islamic-learning app for children. It is made by an independent developer in Brisbane, Australia. We built it so that children can learn safely, and that starts with privacy.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
  • Everything your child does in the app stays on your device.

Information stored on the device

To remember progress, the app saves the following in its private storage on the device. It is never sent anywhere:

  • Learner profiles: the first name or nickname you type, an avatar choice and an age level
  • Learning progress, scores, gold dinars earned and spent, and daily activity used for the parent reports
  • Settings, including the optional parent passcode

You can delete this information at any time by removing a learner in the Grown-ups area, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Android cloud backup of this data is switched off.

Speech and audio

Letters and words are spoken with the text-to-speech engine already on your device (for example, Google Speech Services). The app passes it only the letter or word to be spoken. Qur'an recitations are audio files included inside the app. The app does not use the microphone or camera.

Internet access

The app works offline. It does not send data to us or to any third party. It contains no links to websites, no social features and no way for children to contact other people.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU/UK GDPR.

Parent controls

The Grown-ups area is protected by a passcode that the parent chooses. It lets parents manage learners, view reports and set limits on game time. The passcode is stored only on the device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If we ever wanted to collect any data, we would ask parents first.
